High Five - sample website (preview by Zoetrope Web)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Right-click the zip > Extract All.
2. Open the extracted high-five-site folder and double-click index.html.

index.html is a single self-contained file (about 11 MB, mostly the HD background video): styles, scripts, fonts, icons and the video are all inside it, so it also works if opened on its own. It works offline and loads nothing from the internet. The background video starts when you first scroll; there is a Pause button next to "Roll your own".
The only links that go anywhere are WhatsApp (wa.me) and Call (tel:), both on a placeholder number (27000000000) that does not work yet.

At the bottom of the page there's a small joint roller to play with (grind, load, roll, then tap the lighter to light it). It's just a fun interactive and works with mouse, touch or keyboard. The message that appears after lighting opens WhatsApp on the same placeholder number.

Amber tags mark details still to be supplied: address, hours, WhatsApp and phone numbers, email, licence/permit number and the owner's own story.
